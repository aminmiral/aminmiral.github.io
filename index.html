ERP
Anomaly
Bench

I drove a real Odoo instance — Docker, plus a custom audit addon — through procure-to-pay flows and injected seven fraud typologies into them: duplicate invoices, three-way-match violations, split purchases, subtle overbilling. Every trace and every event came out carrying a ground-truth label.

Then six unsupervised detectors ran against it under leakage-safe temporal splits — Isolation Forest, LOF, one-class SVM, a Markov negative-log-likelihood model, rule-based audit checks. The gap between the two numbers below is the whole point of the project.

Sign
Speak

Real-time Indian Sign Language, both directions. Webcam signs are read as MediaPipe hand landmarks and spoken through a tiered TTS stack — an Indian-voice neural service online, an offline engine when there's no network — while Vosk handles speech-to-text for the hearing side's reply.

No video is ever stored; the pipeline is landmark-only by design. The dynamic word-sign tier runs MediaPipe Holistic into an LSTM over 30-frame sequences, trained with mirror and temporal-dropout augmentation.
